# The English field in the Scale engine's custom rings (2026-11-22)

Test of the author's statement that the English field holds a two-ring set (continuous and discrete) and that the English field sits somewhere in the Scale's custom rings. Script and output: `FourthAgePaper/ScalarContextPropagation/bench/english_field_rings.{py,txt}`; the engine: `ValaQuenta/modules/scale` (`custom_ring_chart`). Data: the shipped `PtolC/monad3_c.bin` (164,283 English words).

**Findings (measured, not tuned):**
* **The stored A-matrix has no direction.** Out-degree equals in-degree for every one of the 164,283 words (corr of the logs 1.000, difference 0.000). The production/comprehension pair (J_pos, J_neg; Broca, Wernicke) is therefore **degenerate in the stored field**: it is one ring, like J_red + J_blue = 1 in the first ring tests. The field records that words met, never in which order, which is also why the learning curve of the A-matrix is flat.
* **A directed model is not degenerate.** In `EnglishStats` (ordered pairs counted), distinct followers and distinct predecessors differ for 24.5% of words, and the asymmetry is the pathway/content structure: *the, a, be, have, not* open many continuations (produce-heavy: *the* 3,004 followers, 1,743 predecessors); *in, as, for, by, it* are reached from many words (comprehension-heavy: *in* 565 followers, 1,198 predecessors). So the two-ring set the author expects (production against comprehension) exists once the order is kept, and it separates the pathway words by function.
* **The other candidate pairs are independent and badly scaled.** E (address energy) with β (learned depth): correlation 0.013; β with A-degree 0.30; E with log fire count −0.005. Folding them raw saturates (|Γ| near 1: the "bad lighting" again, β up to 7.55 against E near 0.0015); on log scales |Γ| is 1.33–1.44 (the active region). No degenerate pair among them.
* **The E column of the shipped bin is not the C monad's E.** It ranges 0.00045 to 0.206 (mean 0.0015), consistent with the spec's `E = |sin(πγ/(γ+1))|` (about π/γ), not the `d* + seed·(A_π − d*)` in [0.246, 0.567] of the C monad and the SemPriCon paper. The Python-built store and the C engine use different energies. To be resolved before the English field is read as the C monad reads it.
* The discrete/continuous split of one number (slot = ⌊N·seed⌋, remainder = the continuous part) exists in the address, but the hash makes the two parts independent by design; the fold of a uniform square shows nothing beyond that.

**Where this leaves the "GR and QM" reading:** the continuous coordinate (E, the spectral energy of a word) and the discrete one (the slot, the count) exist, but the pair that carries meaning is the directed one (production against comprehension), and the stored field has thrown the direction away.
