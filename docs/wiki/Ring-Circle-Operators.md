# Ring–Circle Operators — is the sedenion a set of circle-modifying operators?

**Asked 2026-10-20 (Cody).** Ring theory has hyperspherical representations: add an axis across a ring and rotate in place about it. The universe likes circles. Is Ring Theory the clearest reading of Universal Native Space (UNS) and of 0_RB? Is the sedenion then a set of operators, each deforming, partitioning, permuting or scaling a circle? What is the smallest number of operators of UNS's hypercomplex language? What does each slot, with different bracketings and firing orders, produce?

Everything below is `:CALCULATED`: the repo's own sedenion product (`SedenionSpectralRelativity/emerger_spectrum.py` `cd_mul`, the Cayley–Dickson convention (a₁,a₂)(b₁,b₂) = (a₁b₁ − b₂\*a₂, b₂a₁ + a₂b₁\*), slot k = binary index, e_i e_j = ±e_{i⊕j}), exact integer structure constants, ranks by SVD on integer matrices at tolerance 1e-9, random-phase sampling where stated. Code and results: `VAPMIP/RingCircle/` (`ring_circle_battery.py`, `rc10_engine_per_slot.py`, `rc11_followups.py`, `rc12_euler_circles.py`, `rc_tables.py`; `*.json`). The per-slot table is `RingCircle/slot_table.md` and is reproduced in the SedenionSpectralRelativity wiki page.

## Results

**R1 · every slot is a circle.** For all 15 imaginary slots: L_k² = −I, L_k skew and orthogonal (a complex structure on ℝ¹⁶), R_k² = −I, and span(e₀, e_k) is a closed copy of ℂ. Fifteen circles through the one point e₀ (so cos θ + sin θ e_k is a circle for each k).

**R2 · two circles meet in one of two ways.** Of the 105 pairs of slots, **63** have anticommuting rotation generators (L_iL_j + L_jL_i = 0: perpendicular axes, each rotating the other in place, as i and j do in ℍ) and **42** do not (the anticommutator has Frobenius norm 4√2 for every one of the 42). The 42 are exactly e_a with e_{b+8}, a ≠ b, a and b in 1..7: the 42 Assessors. All 21 pairs inside the octonion slots 1..7 anticommute.

**R3 · how few operators generate the operator ring** (dimension of the associative algebra generated by the left-multiplications on ℝ¹⁶):

| generator circles | dimension |
|---|---|
| {1} | 2 (ℂ) |
| {1,2} | 4 (ℍ) |
| {1,2,4} | 8 |
| {1,2,4,8} | 16 |
| {1,9} (anticommuting pair) | 4 |
| {1,10} (a deforming pair) | 8: one deforming pair doubles the ring |
| {1,2,10} | 16 |
| all 15 L_k | **256 = M₁₆(ℝ)**, the whole endomorphism ring of the sixteen slots |
| inside the octonion, all 7 | 64 = M₈(ℝ) |

**Smallest generating set: five circles.** No subset of four or fewer of the 15 circles generates the full 256 (all 15 + 105 + 455 + 1,365 subsets checked); **1,008 of the 3,003 five-circle subsets do**, for example {1,2,3,9,12}. The four powers of two {1,2,4,8} generate only a 16-dimensional ring (a Clifford algebra); the fifth circle must bring a deforming pair with it.

**R4 · adding an axis across a ring: n circles give rotations of Sⁿ.** Lie closure of the generators, and the dimension of the orbit of e₀:

| circles | Lie algebra | dimension | orbit of e₀ |
|---|---|---|---|
| {1} | so(2) | 1 | 1 (S¹) |
| {1,2} | so(3) | 3 | 3 |
| {1,2,4} | so(4) | 6 | 6 |
| {1,2,4,8} | so(5) | 10 | 10 |
| all 15 | so(16) | 120 | **15 (S¹⁵)** |
| {1,9} | so(3) | 3 | 3 |
| {1,10} (deforming) | — | 5 | 3 |

So the exact statement is: n mutually anticommuting circle operators close to so(n + 1), the rotation group of the next sphere Sⁿ. A ring (1 circle) plus one more axis rotates in place as so(3), plus another so(4), and so on. It is not S^(2ⁿ − 1): three generators give a 6-dimensional orbit, not S⁷; the 15-sphere is reached only when all 15 circles act. A deforming pair adds Lie dimensions the vacuum e₀ does not see (5 against an orbit of 3).

**R5 · rotating in place is exact, even across deforming pairs.** Conjugation of slot j by the circle element u = cos θ + sin θ e_k, in both bracketings, (u e_j)u⁻¹ and u(e_j u⁻¹), equals cos 2θ e_j − sin 2θ (e_j e_k) for **all 210 ordered pairs** (j ≠ k, both imaginary). Every slot is an axis about which every other circle rotates in place. The deformation of the 42 pairs is therefore not in single conjugation; it is in R2's anticommutator, in R3's ring dimension and in R8's composition. (My first guess, that the failing conjugations would be the 42, was wrong; recorded.)

**R6 · where norm is lost.** The product of any two circle elements keeps norm 1 (defect ≤ 2.2e-16, all 210 ordered pairs). For random vectors, |xy|/(|x||y|) = 1 exactly in the 1-, 2-, 4- and 8-dimensional subalgebras and ranges over **[0.626, 1.289]** in 16 dimensions. Zero divisors with two non-zero slots each, x = e_i ± e_j and y = e_k ± e_l: **336 directed pairs** (= ZD_ORDERED_PAIRS), all with i < 8 ≤ j. The engine reads 84 of the 240 vectors e_a ± e_b as zero divisors, all with a < 8 ≤ b.

**R7 · three slots: associative lines and the rest.** All 455 triples of slots multiplied as circle elements, in every one of 3! firing orders and both bracketings, stay on the unit sphere. The number of distinct results: **35 triples give 6** (the 35 lines of PG(3,2): associative, so only the firing order matters), **168 give 8**, **252 give 12** (the maximum 3! × 2). For the generator products, e_a e_b = −e_b e_a; for three generators ((e_a e_b) e_c) = −(e_a (e_b e_c)) in every case: slots 7, 11, 13, 14 alike, so three generators in the sedenion behave as in the octonion.

**R8 · four slots: where the sedenion deforms.** Of 1,365 four-slot sets, 1,155 stay pure rotations under every bracketing and order (sampled phases) and **210 leave the unit sphere**. A deforming set has at least three Assessor pairs inside it (84 sets with 4 pairs and XOR ≠ 0, 84 with 3 pairs, 42 with 4 pairs and XOR = 0), and three Assessor pairs are not sufficient. Four is also the smallest support of a zero-divisor pair (2 + 2). Slot 15 from the four generators {1,2,4,8}: 24 orders × 5 bracketings = 120 results, 60 positive and 60 negative; left comb (((ab)c)d) and (a((bc)d)) carry sign constant +1, ((a(bc))d) and (a(b(cd))) carry −1, and the **balanced bracket ((ab)(cd)) depends on the order beyond parity** for all three pairings {12|48}, {14|28}, {18|24}.

**R9 · the engine's own view of a slot.** In `bracket_firing_engine.py` (31-node tree) the single unit e_k has one non-zero chain; its leaf is the four binary digits of k read as L/R: LLLL = 0 … RRRR = 15. Slot = a four-step address, one binary choice per added axis. Every non-zero node below dimension 16 is an exact isometry (Hurwitz).

**R10 · each prime is a circle.** Slot k is paired with the k-th of the first 16 primes (SedenionSpectralRelativity README). The Euler factor (1 − p^(−½−it))⁻¹ on the critical line traces an exact circle of centre 1/(1 − 1/p) and radius p^(−½)/(1 − 1/p) (maximum deviation 2e-15 for all 16), with angular rate ln p = ln 10 · log₁₀ p per unit t.

**R11 · the UNS operator count.** The repo already names three operators of the log chart in u = ln r: **Add** (shift: diameter, circumference), **Scale** (slope: area), **Sign** (quarter turn u → u + iπ/2): the engine `ValaQuenta/modules/add_scale_sign` (the ASS engine, name locked), wiki 126. Independently, the Lie closure of the radial complex spherical chart's generators gives {J_z} → 1, {J_z, J_y} → 3 (so(3)), adding the dilation D → 4; in ln r the dilation is a translation. Counted as operators: one additive radial generator and two circle generators. Read together: **UNS needs three operator types; the hypercomplex step does not add types, it splits Sign into 15 quarter turns** (each e_k = e^{(π/2)e_k} is the quarter turn of its own circle), and four generating circles (1, 2, 4, 8) reach all 15 slots by bracketing.

## Verdict on the hypothesis

| claim | result |
|---|---|
| ring theory ↔ hyperspheres by adding an axis and rotating in place | **supported exactly** as n circles → so(n+1) → rotations of Sⁿ (R4); **not** as S^(2ⁿ−1) |
| each slot is a circle-modifying operator | supported: 15 circles (R1), every one rotating every other exactly in place (R5) |
| deform | the 42 Assessor pairs, anticommutator 4√2 (R2); 210 four-slot sets (R8) |
| partition | the zero-divisor directions annihilate: 336 directed pairs (R6) |
| permute | the firing order changes sign or result: 6, 8 or 12 distinct results per triple; order-dependent beyond parity at the balanced four-bracket (R7, R8) |
| scale | norm ratio 0.626 … 1.289 outside the octonion (R6) |
| clearest reading of UNS and 0_RB | consistent: every circle passes through e₀, the single point all 15 share, which is 0_RB as e₀ (identity/vacuum); UNS's three operator types (R11). A reading, not a derived identity |

## Limits

Ranks are numerical at 1e-9; "pure" in R7/R8 means no excursion found in sampled phases (4–6 draws), not a proof. The bracketings and firing orders here are parenthesisations and orders of products of circle elements. The bracketing and firing-order engine now carries firing orders on brackets (2026-10-21, `SedenionSpectralRelativity/firing_plan.py`, wiki `Bracketing-and-Firing-Plans.md`). Re-expressed as plans (element reading, every shape over every ordering, circle elements 3/5 + 4/5 e_k, exact), the triple results of R7 reproduce: 35 triples → 6 results, 168 → 8, 252 → 12. The four-slot tables (R8) have not been re-run through the plan engine.
